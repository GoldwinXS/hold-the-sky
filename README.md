# Hold the Sky

A city builder in a country at war. Build a town, keep the power on, and defend it from drones and missiles at night.

**Play it here: https://goldwinxs.github.io/hold-the-sky/**

Three ways to start:

- **Railway halt** (30 nights): a station and a few houses. Grow it into a town while the front comes closer.
- **Zoriv** (20 nights): a city that is already built, and needs defending.
- **Front-line town** (14 nights): close to the front from the first day. Hard.

Best played in a desktop browser with a mouse. Progress is saved in your browser.
